## 这道题在考什么 (Summary)

这是 **FDE 面试的 closer**. 表面问 motivation, 实际筛 5 件事:

- **Specificity** —— 你能 cite specific product / customer / paper, 不是 generic ("OpenAI is leading AI lab")
- **Research depth** —— 你做了功课 (read recent blog post / paper / press release), 不是只看 JD
- **Resume hook alignment** —— 你的具体经历对应该公司具体业务, 不是泛泛 "fit"
- **Differentiation from competitors** —— 你能 articulate 为什么 这家 不是 别家 (并不 bash 别家)
- **Long-term commitment** —— 你想在这家 specifically, 不是 fishing offer

**没说出口的红线**:

- "[Company] is the best AI lab" → generic, bland
- "I want to work on cutting-edge AI" → 任何 lab 都能 apply
- "Your culture is great" → bullshit, no 具体
- "I love [CEO's name]'s book" → 表面 enthusiasm
- 没 cite specific product or customer → 没做功课
- 没 acknowledge trade-off (你 give up 其他 offer) → naive

## 完美答案架构 (Layered Response)

60-90 秒, 5 段:

| # | 层 | 时间 | 这层该说什么 | 开口句 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Specific product / research hook (具体产品 / 研究钩子)** | 20s | Cite specific product / paper / customer | "[OpenAI's Operator paper] / [Anthropic's Claude Code] / [Palantir's Foundry ontology with [customer]]..." |
| 2 | **Why it specifically fits my background (为什么跟我背景特别契合)** | 20s | 你的具体经历对应 | "I built [voice agent agent platform] which directly maps to [Operator's agent stack]..." |
| 3 | **Differentiator vs other AI labs (跟其他 AI 实验室的差异)** | 20s | 不 bash, 但 explicit contrast | "Compared to [other company], [this company] specifically does [X] which is what attracts me..." |
| 4 | **Trade-off acknowledgment (主动认账)** | 10s | 你 explicit know what you give up | "I know [this company] doesn't [Y], I accept because [Z]..." |
| 5 | **Long-term commit signal (长期 commit 信号)** | 10s | 你想 long-term 不是 stepping stone | "I want [this company] long-term, not as fishing offer or stepping stone..." |

Total: ~90 秒.

## ❌ 死路答法 (碰了就挂)

### 死路 1: "[Company] is the best AI lab."

**为什么挂**: Generic, no specifics. Any candidate can say. Interviewer rolls eyes.

**怎么改**: Cite specific product / paper / customer. "OpenAI Operator paper + Stripe customer integration".

---

### 死路 2: "I love your culture."

**为什么挂**: No evidence. Culture is meaningless without specific.

**怎么改**: Don't reference "culture". Reference work shape, product, customer, research.

---

### 死路 3: "I want to work with smart people."

**为什么挂**: Every lab has smart people. Tells nothing.

**怎么改**: Specific. "I want to work with [model team / agent team / customer engineering team] specifically, because my background in [X] aligns".

---

### 死路 4: "I read [CEO]'s book."

**为什么挂**: Surface signal. Reading book ≠ researching company.

**怎么改**: Cite specific blog post / paper / customer case study. Show you researched company direction, not founder.

---

### 死路 5: 没 acknowledge other offers / other companies

**为什么挂**: Naive or dishonest. Anyone applying FDE is interviewing multiple places.

**怎么改**: Honest about competitive process + prefer this company + commit clear signal.

---

### 死路 6: Bash competitor

**为什么挂**: Unprofessional. Everyone in AI industry knows each other.

**怎么改**: Contrast positively. "OpenAI moves faster, Anthropic emphasizes safety. I prefer [the one I'm interviewing for] because of my background in [X]". Not "Anthropic too slow".

---

### 死路 7: 没 trade-off acknowledgment

**为什么挂**: Naive. Every choice has trade-off.

**怎么改**: "[Company] doesn't [Y]. I accept because [Z aligned with my priority]".
