# 🍎 HM Q29 · "Where do you see yourself growing — more research or more engineering/product?"

> **类别**: Fit / Motivation & trajectory · **考点**: 你的成长方向是否天然契合这个 research+product 中间带岗位; 是否真懂自己是哪种工程师 · ⚠️ [✏️] 数字背前替换成真实值

## 🧠 这题在测什么 / 面试官想看到

这题在测 **fit 和留存** — 这个岗位是 research 和 product 的中间带 (把最新模型落成给客户用的 agentic 应用)。面试官怕招到两类人: 纯 research 的人会嫌产品脏活无聊、待不久; 纯 product 工程师又够不到模型/post-training 深度。他们想听到你**主动选择**待在中间, 而且这正是你一直在做的。强答案 = 明确表态 "我成长在 bridging frontier 和 product 的那条带上", 用 POC 角色 + 项目证明这不是迎合而是事实。弱答案 = 含糊 "我都喜欢", 或者偏一端 (露出会嫌弃另一端的信号)。陷阱: 说 "我想做纯 research" → 直接和岗位错位; 说 "我只想交付产品" → 显得够不到技术深度。

## 📋 English Answer (背诵稿, 主答 ~70 秒 + 可延伸)

"I'm clear on this one, and it's a big part of why this role fits. I grow at the seam between frontier work and product — taking what's at the edge of what models can do and turning it into something a real customer actually relies on. I don't want to be purely one or the other, and that's not a compromise answer; it's the work I've deliberately moved toward.

That's literally my current role — I'm a technical POC, which means I sit between the research-grade work and the product. On the voice agent I was doing genuine frontier-adjacent work — multilingual post-training, DPO-style alignment, RL-style optimization over long-horizon agent workflows — but none of it mattered until it shipped to seven markets and moved repayment conversion. The satisfying part for me is precisely that round trip: a post-training technique is interesting, but a post-training technique that a customer feels as a better conversation is the whole point.

So where I want to grow is **deeper on both ends of that bridge, not off it**. On the frontier end, I want to go deeper on post-training and the on-device efficiency frontier — quantization, adapters, the latency-quality-memory tradeoffs. On the product end, I want to get sharper at translating fuzzy customer needs into the right technical bets, faster. The growth I'm chasing isn't 'become a researcher' or 'become a PM' — it's becoming the person a team trusts to take a frontier capability and land it as something real, end to end. That's the muscle, and this role is exactly where it gets stronger."

延伸: "If I'm honest about the failure mode I avoid: I've seen brilliant research that never ships and solid products built on stale models. I want to be the person who refuses both — and that only happens in the middle band, which is where this team operates."

## 🇨🇳 中文要点 (理解 + 记忆骨架)

- **开场明确表态** (不含糊): 我成长在 **frontier 和 product 之间的那条缝** — 把模型能力边缘的东西, 落成客户真正依赖的东西。**这不是折中答案, 是我刻意选的方向**。
- **用 POC 角色证明这是事实, 不是迎合**: 我现在就是 technical POC, 天然坐在 research-grade 工作和产品之间。voice agent 上我做的是真 frontier-adjacent 的活 (multilingual post-training / DPO-style alignment / long-horizon agent 的 RL-style 优化), 但**不上线、不动 conversion 就没意义**。
- **我享受的恰恰是那个 round trip**: 一个 post-training 技巧很有趣, 但**让客户感受到 "对话变好了" 的 post-training 技巧才是全部意义**。
- **成长方向 = 桥的两端都更深, 而不是离开桥**:
  - frontier 端: post-training + on-device efficiency frontier (量化/adapter/latency-quality-memory 取舍)
  - product 端: 更快更准地把模糊客户需求翻成对的技术 bet
  - **不是 "变成 researcher" 或 "变成 PM", 而是变成 "团队信任他把 frontier 能力端到端落地" 的那个人**
- **延伸 (避坑表态)**: 见过惊艳但不上线的 research, 也见过基于过时模型的扎实产品 — 我要做拒绝这两者的人, 而这只能在中间带发生, 正是这个团队所在。
- 记忆钩子: **"长在缝上 / 享受 round trip / 两端都深不离桥 / 拒绝'不上线的research'和'过时的product'"**。

## 🔬 深挖追问 + 答法 (面试官会顺着钻)

**Q: Five years out — do you see yourself as an IC or moving into management?**
"My honest answer is I want to deepen as a technical IC who can lead — which is the path I'm already on, leading a team of three while staying fully hands-on in the post-training and serving stack. I'm not chasing a management title for its own sake; I'd take more scope and more technical leadership, the kind where I'm still close enough to the system to make the hard calls myself. If leading more people is how I get more leverage on landing frontier work, I'd grow into that — but I don't want to grow away from the technology."

**Q: This role has real product-grunt work — eval harnesses, monitoring, latency tuning. Doesn't that get tedious for someone who likes frontier work?**
"That's exactly the work I find satisfying, not tedious — and I'd be honest if it weren't. The eval harness is what makes the frontier work real; without it you're shipping on vibes. I built one for the voice agent by choice, not because someone made me, because I don't trust a model I can't measure. To me, monitoring and latency tuning aren't grunt work standing between me and the interesting part — they *are* the interesting part of making something dependable."

**Q: What would make you feel like you'd outgrown this role?**
"If the work drifted fully to one end — if it became pure research with no shipping, or pure product maintenance on frozen models with no frontier component. As long as the role keeps both ends live — incorporating the latest models and landing them for real customers — I keep growing in it. That two-ended tension is the feature I'm signing up for, not a phase I'm trying to get past."

## ⚠️ 边界 & 红线 (honest limits + what NOT to say)

- **不要偏向纯 research**。说 "我最想做前沿研究" 会和这个 product-facing 岗位直接错位, 暗示你会嫌产品活无聊、待不久。
- **也不要偏向纯交付**。说 "我只在乎把产品做出来" 会让他们怀疑你够不到模型/post-training 深度 (而 JD 明确要 fine-tuning workflows)。
- 别把 "中间带" 说成 "我哪个都行无所谓" 的骑墙。要表达成**主动、刻意的选择**, 有信念 ("拒绝不上线的 research 和过时的 product")。
- 管理/IC 那题诚实: 框成 "想做能 lead 的技术 IC", 别为了显得有野心而说 "想尽快做管理" (会显得想离开技术)。
- 别贬低 product 活 ("eval/monitoring 是脏活但我愿意忍") — 这会露出你其实看不上它。要真心说它就是有趣的部分。

## ✅ 加分钩子 (主动抛, steer 到强项)

- **"这就是我现在的 POC 角色"** — 最强钩子: 把 "成长方向" 和 "现状" 重合, 证明 fit 不是面试话术而是既成事实, 留存风险低。务必主动点出 technical POC 这个身份。
- **"eval harness 是我主动建的, 因为我不信测不了的模型"** — 主动抛, 把 product 活重新定义为信念驱动的 craft, 直击 Apple quality bar, 也命中 JP 的 "LLM eval / monitoring"。
- **桥两端都要更深** — 主动给出具体成长项 (frontier 端的 on-device efficiency / post-training; product 端的需求翻译), 既显示自我认知清晰, 又把 on-device 兴趣 (Q26) 和需求翻译能力 (Q24) 串成一致的人设。
- **"拒绝不上线的 research 和过时的 product"** — 这句有信念感的表态主动抛, 让面试官记住你是 "执着于把前沿落地" 的人, 正是这个团队最需要的画像。
