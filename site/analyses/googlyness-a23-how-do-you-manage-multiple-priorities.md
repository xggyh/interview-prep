# 🟡 G23 · How do you manage multiple priorities? Do you like an environment where priorities keep shifting?

> **信号**: 自我管理 · 对动态环境的真实态度 · **hellointerview 报告数**: 2
> ⚠️ 标 [✏️] 的数字是占位, 背诵前换成你的真实数据.

## 🧠 这题在测什么

一半是方法 (triage 框架), 一半是态度 (Google 环境就是这样 — 答案要真诚的 yes-with-structure).

## 📋 English Answer (背诵稿, ~60-90 秒)

As technical POC I typically run several streams at once — the voice agent, the BNPL chatbot, and platform work, each with different stakeholders and clocks. Two mechanisms keep that sane.
First, an explicit triage hierarchy so prioritization isn't re-litigated daily: production incidents on money-touching systems first, always; then commitments where other teams are blocked on me, because my delay multiplies; then deadline work; then improvements. When everything claims urgency, blast radius and irreversibility break the tie.
Second, I protect deep-work blocks for the one thing each week that actually moves the needle, and batch the coordination load around them — context-switching is where multi-stream work quietly dies. And I over-communicate state: stakeholders always know where their thread stands, which prevents the panic-driven reshuffles that fake urgency creates [✏️ 细节按真实习惯调整].
Do I like shifting priorities? Honestly, yes — with one condition: the shifts should come from learning, not from chaos. A pivot because eval data or a customer taught us something is the job. I'd rather have that than a frozen roadmap; my whole career — CV to voice to agents — is basically priorities shifting toward where the value moved.

## 🇨🇳 中文完整版 (口播稿, ~60-90 秒)

作为 technical POC 我通常同时跑几条线 — voice agent、BNPL chatbot、platform, 各自有不同的 stakeholder 和节奏. 两个机制让它可持续.
第一, 显式的 triage 层级, 让优先级不用每天重新吵一遍: 动钱系统的生产事故永远第一; 其次是别的团队被我阻塞的承诺, 因为我的延迟会放大; 然后是 deadline 工作; 最后是改进项. 当所有事都喊紧急时, 用爆炸半径和不可逆性来打破平局.
第二, 我保护深度工作块 — 每周真正推动进展的那一件事 — 把协调类工作打包在周边; 上下文切换是多线工作悄悄死掉的地方. 同时我会过度沟通状态: stakeholder 永远知道自己那条线进展到哪, 这能防止假紧急引发的恐慌式重排 [✏️ 细节按真实习惯调整].
喜不喜欢优先级一直变? 说实话, 喜欢 — 但有一个条件: 变化应该来自学习, 而不是混乱. 因为 eval 数据或客户教了我们新东西而 pivot, 这就是工作本身. 我的整个职业路径 — CV 到语音到 agent — 本来就是优先级随价值迁移的过程.

## 🇨🇳 中文速记 (结构记忆)

POC 同时跑 voice/BNPL/platform. 两机制: ①显式 triage 层级 (动钱事故>别队被我阻塞>deadline>改进; 平局看爆炸半径+不可逆性) ②保护深工作块+批量协调+过度沟通状态防假紧急. 态度: 真心喜欢变化, 条件是变化来自学习而非混乱 — 我的职业 (CV→voice→agent) 本身就是优先级随价值迁移.

## 🔁 高概率追问

**Q: Tell me about dropping a ball while juggling.**

Early on I silently absorbed a platform request during a voice-agent crunch and delivered late. Lesson: renegotiate explicitly the moment capacity changes — a fast honest 'not this week' beats a quiet slip.
