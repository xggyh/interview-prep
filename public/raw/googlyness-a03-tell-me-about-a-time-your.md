# 🟡 G3 · Tell me about a time your manager set reasonable demands. Follow-up: when demands were unreasonable.

> **信号**: 向上管理 · 有方案的拒绝 · **hellointerview 报告数**: 3
> ⚠️ 标 [✏️] 的数字是占位, 背诵前换成你的真实数据.

## 🧠 这题在测什么

双面题: 接得住高目标 (reasonable), 也敢对不合理 timeline 说不 — 且 pushback 必须带替代方案.

## 📋 English Answer (背诵稿, ~60-90 秒)

Reasonable: my manager set an aggressive end-to-end latency target for the voice agent — in a live phone call, silence kills the interaction. He fixed the outcome but gave me full freedom on method. I led the streaming-architecture upgrade: streaming ASR partials, sentence-level TTS chunking, packetization and interruption-handling tuning. We hit the bar [✏️ 填真实前后毫秒数]. That's healthy pressure: hard target, open path.
The unreasonable follow-up: leadership later wanted the agent live in every remaining market within a single quarter — which compressed per-market language adaptation, compliance review and UAT below what our first three launches proved was the floor [✏️ 周数核实].
I didn't refuse bare-handed. I brought the launch data, showed the irreducible per-market steps, and proposed a phased plan: two markets done properly, plus a reusable launch checklist that would make each next market faster. My manager took that upstairs and the phased plan won.
My rule on unreasonable asks: never just push back — return a plan that still serves their underlying goal.

## 🇨🇳 中文完整版 (口播稿, ~60-90 秒)

合理的部分: 我老板给 voice agent 定了一个很激进的端到端延迟目标 — 电话里沉默是杀手. 他定死结果, 但方法完全放权给我. 我主导了 streaming 架构升级: ASR partial 流式输出、TTS 按句分块、packetization 和打断处理的调优, 最后达标 [✏️ 填真实前后毫秒]. 这是健康的压力: 目标硬, 路径开放.
不合理的 follow-up: 后来 leadership 要求剩余所有市场一个季度内全部上线, 这会把每个市场的语言适配、合规审查和 UAT 压缩到我们前三次上线证明过的下限以下 [✏️ 周数核实].
我没有空手说不. 我带着上线数据回去, 展示了每市场不可压缩的步骤, 然后提了分阶段方案: 先把两个市场做扎实, 同时产出一份可复用的上线 checklist, 让后续每个市场更快. 老板拿这个方案向上谈, 分阶段方案通过了.
我对不合理要求的原则: 拒绝必须带替代方案, 而且方案要保住对方的底层目标.

## 🇨🇳 中文速记 (结构记忆)

合理: latency 硬指标+方法自由 → streaming 改造达标. 不合理: 一季度全市场 → 用前三市场数据证明硬下限, 给 phased+checklist 替代案. 拒绝必带方案.

## 🔁 高概率追问

**Q: And if leadership had insisted anyway?**

Disagree and commit — ship the two safest markets first, put the compressed-review risk in writing, and make the tradeoff visible rather than silent.
