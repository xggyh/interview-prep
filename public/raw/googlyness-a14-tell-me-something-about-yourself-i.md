# 🟡 G14 · Tell me something about yourself I won't find on your resume.

> **信号**: 真实感 · 用户共情 · **hellointerview 报告数**: 1
> ⚠️ 标 [✏️] 的数字是占位, 背诵前换成你的真实数据.

## 🧠 这题在测什么

别报第二份简历. 给一个有性格的工作习惯, 且能解释你为什么是这样的工程师.

## 📋 English Answer (背诵稿, ~60-90 秒)

Something that's shaped how I build more than any course: I regularly sit in customer-operations call centers and listen to live collection and support calls — headphones on, next to the agents, taking notes.
It started as launch-week diligence for the voice agent, but I kept going back, because nothing in our dashboards taught me what those sessions did. A metric tells me a call failed; sitting there tells me *why* — the user was driving, the user put the phone down to dig out their ID, the user code-switched mid-sentence, the human agent defused anger with a pause our bot would have talked straight through.
Several of the things I'm proudest of shipping — better interruption handling, the OTP retry posture, the escalation phrasing for distressed users — trace back to specific calls I overheard, not to any analysis I ran.
I'd describe it as refusing to let the data pipeline be my only contact with reality. Models compress reality; the call center is where I go to decompress it. That instinct — go sit where the user actually is — is the part of me that doesn't fit in a skills section.

## 🇨🇳 中文完整版 (口播稿, ~60-90 秒)

有一个塑造我工程方式、但不在简历上的习惯: 我会定期去客服运营的呼叫中心, 戴上耳机坐在坐席旁边, 听真实的催收和客服通话, 做笔记.
最开始是 voice agent 上线周的尽调, 但后来我一直在去 — 因为仪表盘教不了我那些现场能教的东西. 指标告诉我一通电话失败了; 坐在那里告诉我「为什么」— 用户在开车、用户放下手机去找证件、用户半句话切换语言、人工坐席用一个停顿化解了愤怒而我们的 bot 会直接说下去.
我最自豪的几个交付 — 更好的打断处理、OTP 流程的重试姿态、对情绪激动用户的安抚话术 — 都能追溯到我亲耳听到的具体通话, 而不是任何一次数据分析.
我把它描述为: 拒绝让数据管道成为我和现实的唯一接触面. 模型是对现实的压缩; 呼叫中心是我去解压的地方. 如果加入 Google 做 FDE, 这种「去用户真实所在的地方坐下来」的本能, 就是简历技能栏里装不下的那部分我.

## 🇨🇳 中文速记 (结构记忆)

习惯: 定期去客服中心听真实通话. 仪表盘说失败, 现场告诉你为什么 (开车/放下手机找证件/中途换语/人工用停顿化解愤怒). 中断处理/OTP retry/安抚话术都来自具体通话. 金句: 模型压缩现实, 呼叫中心是我解压的地方.

## 🔁 高概率追问

**Q: How does that change your engineering?**

It reorders my backlog — what looks like an edge case in aggregate data often dominates real user pain, and that's where the highest-ROI fixes have repeatedly been.
