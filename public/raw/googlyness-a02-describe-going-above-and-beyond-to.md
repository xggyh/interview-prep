# 🟡 G2 · Describe going above and beyond to help someone.

> **信号**: 协作 · 杠杆思维 · no-not-my-job · **hellointerview 报告数**: 6
> ⚠️ 标 [✏️] 的数字是占位, 背诵前换成你的真实数据.

## 🧠 这题在测什么

测两点: 是否把别队的成功当自己的事; 帮忙是否有杠杆 (沉淀成可复用资产) 而非纯加班.

## 📋 English Answer (背诵稿, ~60-90 秒)

A sister team in Global Payment was making their first GenAI launch on our internal Agent Platform, and two weeks before their deadline their workflow kept failing in staging. Not my project, nobody asked — but they were the platform's first external adopter; if their launch slipped, cross-team adoption would stall with it.
I paired with their engineer over several evenings. The root cause was a tool-schema mismatch our docs never covered. I made a point of debugging it *with* him rather than for him — then turned the pain into platform assets: a troubleshooting runbook, plus schema validation at tool-registration time so that whole error class now fails fast with a clear message instead of dying in staging.
They launched on schedule [✏️ 核实], and the next teams onboarding never hit the issue.
How I think about it: fixing someone's bug is kindness; converting their bug into a platform guardrail is leverage. I aim for the second every time.

## 🇨🇳 中文速记 (结构记忆)

兄弟团队首个平台落地卡 staging; 主动晚间 pair debug; 不只修 — 沉淀 runbook + 注册时 schema 校验; '修 bug 是好心, 把 bug 变护栏是杠杆'.

## 🔁 高概率追问

**Q: How do you balance this against your own work?**

Time-boxed to evenings and told my lead transparently — if it had needed days, I'd renegotiate priorities explicitly rather than silently absorb it.
