🧠 这题在测什么

别报第二份简历. 给一个有性格的工作习惯, 且能解释你为什么是这样的工程师.

📋 English Answer (背诵稿, ~60-90 秒)

Something that's shaped how I build more than any course: I regularly sit in customer-operations call centers and listen to live collection and support calls — headphones on, next to the agents, taking notes. It started as launch-week diligence for the voice agent, but I kept going back, because nothing in our dashboards taught me what those sessions did. A metric tells me a call failed; sitting there tells me why — the user was driving, the user put the phone down to dig out their ID, the user code-switched mid-sentence, the human agent defused anger with a pause our bot would have talked straight through. Several of the things I'm proudest of shipping — better interruption handling, the OTP retry posture, the escalation phrasing for distressed users — trace back to specific calls I overheard, not to any analysis I ran. I'd describe it as refusing to let the data pipeline be my only contact with reality. Models compress reality; the call center is where I go to decompress it. That instinct — go sit where the user actually is — is the part of me that doesn't fit in a skills section.

🇨🇳 中文速记 (结构记忆)

习惯: 定期去客服中心听真实通话. 仪表盘说失败, 现场告诉你为什么 (开车/放下手机找证件/中途换语/人工用停顿化解愤怒). 中断处理/OTP retry/安抚话术都来自具体通话. 金句: 模型压缩现实, 呼叫中心是我解压的地方.

🔁 高概率追问

Q: How does that change your engineering?

It reorders my backlog — what looks like an edge case in aggregate data often dominates real user pain, and that's where the highest-ROI fixes have repeatedly been.
