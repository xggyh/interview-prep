🧠 这题在测什么

与 Q1 准备不同故事 — 同一轮可能两题都被问. 这里用跨职能 (非工程师) 冲突.

📋 English Answer (背诵稿, ~60-90 秒)

Marketing wanted the BNPL chatbot to proactively pitch PayLater in every conversation, whatever the user asked. I pushed back: in several of our markets the regulatory posture required BNPL promotion to be opt-in, and bolting sales onto a support conversation risked the user trust the bot depended on. But I didn't open with "no." I opened with their goal — they were under real conversion pressure, and that was legitimate. So I proposed making the disagreement empirical: a two-week A/B. The hard-upsell variant did lift conversion short-term, but churn and complaint signals moved the wrong way, and compliance flagged the passive-promotion pattern [✏️ 数字核实]. I brought back the data with an alternative that still served their goal: contextual suggestion — surfacing PayLater only when the user's own question made it relevant. They took it; the soft variant kept most of the lift without the trust and compliance cost. What actually resolved it: treating their objective as valid, converting opinion into experiment, and offering an option where they didn't have to lose.

🇨🇳 中文完整版 (口播稿, ~60-90 秒)

Marketing 想让 BNPL chatbot 在每轮对话里主动推销 PayLater, 不管用户问什么. 我反对: 我们好几个市场的监管姿态要求 BNPL 推广必须 opt-in, 而且把销售硬塞进客服对话, 会烧掉 bot 赖以生存的用户信任. 但我没有用「不行」开场, 而是先认可他们的目标 — 他们背着真实的转化压力, 这是正当的. 所以我提议把分歧变成实验: 两周 A/B. 硬推版本短期转化确实涨了, 但 churn 和投诉信号全部恶化, 合规也标记了被动推销模式 [✏️ 数字核实]. 我带着数据回去, 同时带了一个仍然服务他们目标的替代方案: 上下文相关推荐 — 只在用户自己的问题让 PayLater 真正相关时才出现. 他们接受了; 软推版本保住了大部分转化提升, 没有信任和合规的代价. 真正化解冲突的是三件事: 把对方的目标当真, 把观点之争变成实验, 以及给出一个对方不用输的选项.

🇨🇳 中文速记 (结构记忆)

Marketing 要每轮硬推 PayLater vs 监管 opt-in 红线; 先认可其转化压力 → 2 周 A/B; 硬推短期转化↑但 churn/投诉/合规↓; 带数据+替代案 (上下文相关才推荐). 解法=认可目标+经验化分歧+给不输的选项.

🔁 高概率追问

Q: What if the A/B had favored hard upsell everywhere?

The compliance line still binds — I'd have said upfront that metrics tune the tradeoff but regulatory red lines aren't A/B-testable.
