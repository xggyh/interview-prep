我的思路与解法

🍎 HM Q24 · "Tell me about partnering with non-technical / business / product stakeholders."

类别: Behavioral / Cross-functional collaboration · 考点: 能否把模糊的业务/合规需求翻译成工程指标, 并持续对齐 — 这是 Gao 的已知软肋, 同时是 JD 重点, 必须打成强项 · ⚠️ [✏️] 数字背前替换成真实值

🧠 这题在测什么 / 面试官想看到

Customer Systems 团队就是给 Apple 的业务线做 GenAI 应用 — 你永远在和产品、运营、合规、客服业务方合作, 工程不是终点。面试官想看到: 你能把一句模糊的业务诉求 ("催收要更有效率") 翻译成可度量的工程目标, 能管理预期, 能在冲突时既守工程底线又服务业务。强答案 = 一个具体的翻译动作 + 一个常态化的对齐机制 + 一次你和业务方意见不一致时如何收敛。弱答案 = "我和 PM 开会沟通需求" 这种空话, 或者把业务方当成只会提需求的甲方。陷阱: 显得不耐烦/技术傲慢 ("业务方不懂技术") → Apple 极其反感这种姿态。

📋 English Answer (背诵稿, 主答 ~80 秒 + 可延伸)

"My voice agent project was driven almost entirely by non-technical partners, so this is something I do constantly. Debt collection is a compliance-and-business problem first, and an engineering problem second — so I couldn't just build what I thought was cool. My main partners were the collections operations team and the compliance team, neither of which speaks in metrics or model terms.

The core skill I had to develop was translating fuzzy business asks into engineering metrics. A concrete example: ops came to me and said 'the agent feels too pushy, customers are hanging up.' That's not a spec — I can't optimize 'pushy.' So I sat with them, listened to a batch of real call recordings together, and we converted 'pushy' into three measurable things: early-hangup rate, number of repayment asks per call, and a tone-compliance score the compliance team could actually sign off on. Once it was numbers, I could put it in the eval harness and optimize it. That translation step — turning a feeling into a metric — is the part I think a lot of engineers skip, and it's where the real partnership happens.

I also made the alignment structural, not ad hoc. We ran a weekly review where I'd bring the metric movements and a few real transcripts, and ops and compliance would tell me where the agent was off. That meeting did two things: it kept their fuzzy goals continuously translated into my backlog, and it kept their expectations calibrated — they could see the tradeoffs instead of just asking for everything at once.

Where it got real was when compliance wanted a hard rule the agent could never violate, and ops wanted maximum repayment conversion — those pull against each other. I didn't pick a side. I framed it as a constraint-then-optimize problem: compliance rules are non-negotiable guardrails, and inside those guardrails I optimize conversion as hard as I can. That reframing let both sides say yes, because nobody had to lose."

延伸: "Earlier, on the BNPL chatbot, I also defined the metrics and the eval harness jointly with product and ops from day one — so this pattern of co-owning the definition of success with business partners is something I've now done across two projects."

🇨🇳 中文完整版 (口播稿, 与英文对应)

"我那个 voice agent 项目, 基本上全程是被非技术的合作方驱动的, 所以这件事我天天在做。催收首先是一个合规和业务问题, 其次才是工程问题 — 所以我不能光做我自己觉得 cool 的东西。我主要的合作方是 collections 运营团队和合规团队, 这两边都不讲指标、也不讲模型那套话。

我必须练出来的核心能力, 就是把模糊的业务诉求翻译成工程指标。举个具体的例子: 运营来找我, 说 '这个 agent 感觉太 pushy 了, 客户都挂电话'。这不是一个 spec — 我没法去优化 'pushy' 这个词。所以我就跟他们坐下来, 一起听了一批真实的通话录音, 我们把 'pushy' 拆成了三个可度量的东西: early-hangup rate (早挂率)、每通电话催还款的次数、还有一个合规团队真的能签字认可的 tone-compliance score。一旦它变成数字, 我就能把它放进 eval harness 去优化。这个翻译的动作 — 把一种感觉变成一个指标 — 我觉得是很多工程师会跳过的一步, 而真正的合作恰恰发生在这一步上。

我还把这种对齐做成了结构化的, 而不是临时的。我们跑了一个每周的 review, 我会带上指标的变化, 再加几段真实的 transcript, 然后运营和合规告诉我 agent 哪里不对。这个会有两个作用: 一是它让他们那些模糊的目标持续地被翻译进我的 backlog; 二是它让他们的预期一直被校准 — 他们能看见取舍, 而不是一次性就把所有东西都要过去。

真正见真章的时刻, 是合规想要一条 agent 永远不能违反的硬规则, 而运营想要最大化的还款 conversion — 这两个是互相拉扯的。我没有选边站。我把它 reframe 成一个 '先约束、再优化' 的问题: 合规规则是不可谈判的 guardrail, 然后在这个 guardrail 之内, 我把 conversion 往死里优化。这么一重新框, 两边都能说 yes, 因为没有人需要输。"

延伸: "更早的时候, 在那个 BNPL chatbot 上, 我也是从第一天起就和产品、运营一起定义指标和 eval harness 的 — 所以这种和业务方共同 own '成功的定义' 的模式, 我现在已经在两个项目上都做过了。"

🔬 深挖追问 + 答法 (面试官会顺着钻, Apple 钻到边界)

Q: How did you handle it when a business partner asked for something technically infeasible or a bad idea? "I don't say 'no, that's impossible' — that ends the conversation. I show the tradeoff with data. Ops once wanted the agent to push repayment harder on every call; I pulled the early-hangup numbers and showed that past a certain aggressiveness, conversion actually dropped because customers hung up. Once they saw the curve, they redefined the ask themselves. My job is to make the tradeoff visible, then let the business own the call within the guardrails."

Q: Compliance and ops disagreed — how did you actually get to a decision, not just reframe it? "The reframe set the structure, but the decision needed an owner. I made compliance rules a hard gate in the eval — any candidate model that violated them was disqualified before conversion was even scored. That made it unambiguous: we never trade compliance for conversion, full stop. Within that, ops owned the conversion target. So the decision rule was explicit and written down, not relitigated every week."

Q: How do you make sure a non-technical partner actually understands the tradeoff, not just nods along? "I stopped using metrics in the abstract and started bringing real transcripts to every review. A number like 'tone score dropped two points' means nothing to ops; a transcript where the agent sounds rude is instantly obvious to them. Grounding every metric in a concrete example they can judge themselves is how I know the understanding is real and not polite nodding."

⚠️ 边界 & 红线 (honest limits + what NOT to say)

  • 绝不流露技术傲慢。不要说 "业务方不懂技术 / 需求乱提"。一旦有这个味道, Apple 直接扣分。框成 "他们懂业务我懂工程, 合作发生在翻译层"。
  • 这是已知软肋, 所以别试图装成天生擅长沟通的人。框成 "我刻意发展出来的一套方法 (翻译 + 每周机制 + 约束优化)" — 方法论比性格更可信, 也更像工程师该有的答案。
  • 别把 "对齐" 说成 "开会沟通" 这种空话。一定要落到具体机制 (weekly review + 带 transcript + 写死的 decision rule)。
  • 冲突那段不要把自己塑造成 "和事佬"。是 "我设计了一个让双方都能 yes 的结构", 是 ownership, 不是妥协。

✅ 加分钩子 (主动抛, steer 到强项)

  • "compliance-first" 思维 — 主动强调催收/支付场景里合规是硬约束, 正好呼应 Apple 对 privacy / 用户信任 / guardrail 的极度重视。这是把 fintech 背景转成 Apple 文化加分的最佳钩子。
  • co-own the definition of success — 主动点出 BNPL 和 voice agent 两个项目都是和产品/运营一起定义指标和 eval harness, 证明这是稳定模式不是一次性运气。直接命中 JD 的 "LLM eval"。
  • 把模糊翻成指标的能力 — 这正是 research↔product 中间带 (POC 角色) 的核心能力, 可顺势衔接 Q29 (成长方向) 和 Q27 (多市场)。
  • 主动提 "带真实 transcript 对齐" 这个细节 — 体现 attention to detail, Apple 喜欢这种具体到 craft 的做法。
