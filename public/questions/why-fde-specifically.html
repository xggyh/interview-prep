Problem Statement

[OpenAI Recruiter] Why do you want FDE specifically versus other engineering roles at OpenAI / Google / etc?

我的思路与解法

题目（verbatim）

[Recruiter Screen 30-45 min]

"Why do you want FDE specifically versus other engineering roles at OpenAI / Google / etc?"

出处：OpenAI recruiter 经典开场, Google FDE recruiter / Tomoro / Palantir 也都问.

Round：Recruiter Screen (30-45 min)

这道题在考什么

Recruiter screen 不是技术深度, 是 fit + clarity：

  1. 能不能 5 句话说清 —— 啰嗦 = 没想清
  2. 你懂 FDE vs others 区别 —— 否则候选 mistake apply
  3. Motivation 真不真
  4. 未来 trajectory —— FDE 是 stepping stone 还是 home

FDE vs others 区分（recruiter 关心的）

RoleWhat适合 person
Pure ML / ResearchFrontier model 训练Loves papers, novel architectures
Platform Engineer内部 infra (vLLM tuning)Loves systems
Software Engineer (SWE)Product featuresLoves shipping
FDEAt customer site, deploy + customize + own outcomeLoves customer-facing impact + technical depth
Sales EngineerPre-sales technicalCustomer-facing but less hands-on coding
Customer SuccessPost-sales relationshipLess technical

FDE 区别：both deep coding AND on-site with customer，不是 either-or。

60-90 秒 answer 框架

段What
1 (10s)Identity anchor
2 (20s)What specifically draws you to FDE
3 (20s)Why you (proof)
4 (15s)Why this company specifically
5 (10s)Forward-looking (career arc)

你专属 sample answer（基于 resume）

"Three reasons FDE specifically, not other engineering roles.

One: I love the gap between research and production. I've been at TikTok Payment for 3 years building production GenAI systems — voice agents, RAG chatbots, agent platforms. The most fulfilling work is when frontier model capability meets a specific business constraint at a customer site and I have to bridge the gap with engineering craft, not just model selection. That's the FDE job description.

Two: I've effectively been doing FDE work without the title. At TikTok I'm 'Expert Algorithm Engineer + Technical POC'. The POC part means I sit between research, engineering, product, and regional business teams across 7 markets. I translate business asks to model deliverables, debug at customer-equivalent sites (regional ops teams), and own outcome metrics like repayment conversion. The role I'm doing today is FDE shaped — I want the title and team that matches.

Three: At Tomoro / OpenAI Deployment Company specifically, FDEs work with frontier-model researchers down the hall, not 6 layers of corporate away. For someone who's been doing post-training (SFT, DPO, RL alignment) and inference optimization (vLLM, SGLang) on internal models, working at the source is irresistible.

Not pure research because I'm motivated by business outcome, not papers. Not pure SWE because I want customer interaction, not isolated feature work. Not sales engineering because I want to own the code, not the slides.

FDE is exactly the middle of those three."

⏱️ 75 seconds.

5 个 "what to NOT say"

❌ "I want to work at OpenAI, FDE is the most open role" → 不是 fit, 是 opportunism ❌ "Better comp than other roles" → 真实但 surface ❌ "I don't want to do research anymore" → defining negatively ❌ "My friend works there" → social, not personal ❌ "It's a hot new field" → trend-chasing

5 个 "do say" formulas

✅ "I've been doing FDE work without the title" + concrete evidence ✅ "What energizes me: [specific scenario]" ✅ "At [target company], FDE specifically means [X], which is [why fit]" ✅ "My experience at [current role] maps onto [FDE responsibility]" ✅ "FDE is the middle of [three other roles I considered]"

简历专属 reframe

你完全可以用上面 sample 直接 say. 关键 anchors:

  • "7 markets, 6 languages" (voice agent) — multi-stakeholder deployment proof
  • "Translate business asks to model deliverables" — FDE 灵魂
  • "Post-training + inference (vLLM/SGLang)" — technical depth match
  • "Indonesia refund tier scoping" — concrete customer-facing story
  • "Expert Algorithm Engineer + Technical POC" — title evidence

5 个 recruiter follow-ups

Q1: "Why now? You've been at TikTok 3 years." A: "Two things converging. One, my current work is FDE-shaped but TikTok doesn't have an FDE org — so the role doesn't compound. Two, GenAI deployment is at an inflection point — frontier model capability changing what's feasible monthly, and being at OpenAI's deployment arm puts me at the center of that change. The optimal time to make this move is now, before the field stabilizes."

Q2: "Why not just internal transfer to a research-y team?" A: "I've considered it. The trade-off: TikTok's strength is consumer-scale infra, which is amazing but not where frontier models are built. To compound on frontier-model knowledge, the right place is at a frontier lab. Plus I want deployment at customer sites (vs deployment within one company), and Tomoro is that exact role."

Q3: "You'd be working with smaller teams. Comfortable with that?" A: "Yes — my team is 3 engineers now, and many TikTok teams I work with are < 10. I prefer it. Smaller teams mean direct ownership and faster iteration. Less politics, more shipping."

Q4: "Comp at OpenAI is competitive — what's your target?" A: "I'd want to understand the structure first — base, equity (especially PPU now that OpenAI Deployment Company is established), and remote / Singapore / London considerations. Based on what I've seen for senior FDE at OpenAI, total comp in the $300-500k USD range with the right mix of base + PPU equity would let me make this move. Can you share what you've budgeted for this level?"

Q5: "What if we offer SWE-Production role instead of FDE?" A: Honest:

  • "Depends on the role specifics. If SWE-Production is internal team, no customer interaction, it's a less good fit for me — I'd be giving up the customer-facing piece I want."
  • "If it's a hybrid with some customer aspect, I'd want to understand the day-to-day. I'm flexible on title if the shape of the work matches what I described."

❌ 易错点

  1. List of generic reasons — "love AI, want to learn" — bland
  2. Defining by negation — "don't want research" only
  3. Naming wrong differentiator — "FDE = remote work" misses the role
  4. Too short — < 30s feels rehearsed-shallow
  5. Too long — > 2min loses recruiter attention
  6. No specific company connection — generic FDE pitch
  7. Forward-looking too vague — "I want to grow"

✅ 加分项

  1. "I've been doing FDE without the title" — show, don't tell
  2. 3 specific reasons with proof each
  3. FDE vs adjacent roles distinction
  4. Company-specific draw (Tomoro acquisition / Vertex AI scale)
  5. Career arc clearly articulated
  6. Acknowledge tradeoffs (not pure research)
  7. Quote prior FDE-shaped story (Indonesia refund tier)

Cheat Sheet

60-90 sec structure:
  (10s) Identity anchor
  (20s) What draws you to FDE
  (20s) Why you (proof)
  (15s) Why this company
  (10s) Career arc forward

3 reasons formula:
  1. Bridge research-to-production (motivation)
  2. Already doing FDE work without title (proof)
  3. Company-specific (frontier model proximity)

Anchors to use:
  - "7 markets, 6 languages" (multi-stakeholder)
  - "Expert + Technical POC" (FDE-shaped already)
  - "Indonesia refund tier scoping" (customer-facing story)
  - "Post-training + vLLM/SGLang" (deep technical)
  - "Translate business asks" (FDE soul)

FDE vs others positioning:
  Not pure research — care about business outcome
  Not pure SWE — want customer interaction
  Not sales engineer — own the code, not slides
  FDE = middle of the three

Don't say:
  - "I love AI"
  - "Better comp"
  - "I'm bored at TikTok"
  - "My friend works here"
  - "Hot new field"

Salary anchor (if asked):
  $300-500k total, right base + equity mix
  Reverse question: "what's your budget?"
